# Sky365 Engineering Glossary

This file defines official terms used across Sky365 projects. Project-local documents may extend this glossary but should not silently redefine these terms.

## Agent

A software actor that uses models, tools, policies, memory, and workflows to perform bounded tasks.

## Tool

A callable capability exposed to an agent, with explicit inputs, outputs, permissions, and audit behavior.

## Runtime

The execution environment that coordinates model calls, tools, state, policies, retries, and outputs.

## Workspace

A logical working boundary containing users, configuration, knowledge, sessions, resources, and permissions.

## Tenant

An isolated customer or organizational boundary in a multi-tenant system.

## Module

A cohesive product or domain capability with explicit responsibilities and interfaces.

## Semantic Layer

A structured representation of meaning, entities, measures, business concepts, and relationships used to make data and knowledge interpretable across systems.

## Concept Graph

A graph of concepts and their relationships, used for navigation, reasoning, retrieval, and knowledge organization.

## RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation: retrieval of relevant evidence that is supplied to a generative model for grounded output.

## ADR

Architecture Decision Record: a durable record of context, decision, alternatives, and consequences for a significant engineering choice.

## As-Is Architecture

The evidence-based description of the system as it currently operates.

## To-Be Architecture

A proposed future design. It must never be presented as current behavior.

## Checkpoint

The durable record of where work stopped, what was verified, what remains unknown, and the exact next action.
